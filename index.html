How it works

1

Pick your sources

Paste any site's feed, browse 1,250 hand-sorted feeds across 67 topics, or bring your list from another reader as an OPML file.

2

Read your edition

Each source gets its own section with its five newest posts. Drag sections into the order you like to read them. Pull down to refresh.

3

Keep the good bits

Share a passage from your browser to Morning Brief and it's saved as a clipping, with a link back to the exact sentence.

What this paper won't do

  • Decide what you see. There's no ranking and no "for you". Just your sources, in your order.
  • Sell your attention. No ads, no sponsored posts, no "recommended" anything.
  • Keep a file on you. It doesn't record what you open or read. There's no analytics at all.
  • Scroll forever. Five posts a source, then the edition ends. You're allowed to stop.

The small print

Works offline. Once your edition has loaded, it's still there on the train.

Installs like an app. Add it to your home screen on your phone or laptop.

Syncs if you want it to. Sign in with an email link to keep your sources on every device. Not required.

Light and dark editions. It follows your device, or you can pick.

Your list stays yours. Export your sources as OPML any time and take them to any reader.

Plain about privacy. What's stored and why is all on one short page.
