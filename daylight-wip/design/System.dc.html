The sun

Same clay lighting as the Open Loops mascot, and the pink dot carries over. Open Loops is an open ring; Morning Brief is a sun on a horizon. One closes, one rises.

Rules

Color means a sourceOnly the source tags carry color. Headlines, cards and buttons stay cream and ink.
Your order decidesNothing is ranked. Sections follow your source order, and each opens with that source's newest story.
Read on their siteEvery story opens on the publisher's own page. Morning Brief is the front page, not the paper.
