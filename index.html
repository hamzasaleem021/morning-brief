How it works

Pick your sources. Read what's new. Keep the good bits.

  1. 1

    Pick your sources

    Browse 1,250 hand-sorted feeds across 67 topics, paste any website and Morning Brief finds its feed, or bring your list from another reader as an OPML file.

  2. 2

    Read your brief

    Each source gets a section, in the order you set, opening with its newest story. You see what's new since you last looked. Every story opens on the writer's own site.

  3. 3

    Keep the good bits

    Share a passage from your browser to Morning Brief and it's saved as a clipping, with a link back to the exact sentence.

What it won't do

A reader with a few firm rules.

  • Decide what you see.There's no ranking and no "for you". Just your sources, in your order.
  • Sell your attention.No ads, no sponsored posts, no "recommended" anything.
  • Keep a file on you.What you've opened is remembered on your device only, so it can grey it out. Nothing about your reading leaves it. No analytics at all.
  • Scroll forever.Once you've seen what's new, the brief ends. You're allowed to stop.
