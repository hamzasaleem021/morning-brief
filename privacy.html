Privacy

Your data, your call.

What Morning Brief stores, where it lives, and how to delete it.

We store only what's needed to make Morning Brief work on your devices. No behavioral tracking, no ads, no data sales.

On your device

These stay in your browser on this device and never leave it. Clearing your browser data removes them.

  • Your sources: name, feed address, order, color, and whether each one shows in Today.
  • Your theme: Auto, Day or Night.
  • When you last had the app open, and how many times you've opened it. These decide what counts as new and when to suggest installing the app.
  • Which stories you've already seen and which you've opened, stored as short fingerprints rather than titles or links. This is what lets the brief show what's new and grey out what you've read.
  • Two counts, how many briefs you've had and how many stories you've opened, and which of the small cards at the end of a brief you've already answered. These decide when a card shows, and it never shows again once you've answered it.
  • The last article you opened from your brief, for up to six hours, so a passage you share from it can be matched to the right source.
  • A copy of your clippings and of the source catalog, so both work offline.

In the cloud, only if you sign in

  • Your email address, to send you the sign-in link.
  • Your source list, including order, names and which sources are switched off, so it appears on your other devices.
  • Your clippings: the passage, your note, the article's link and title, its source, and when you saved it.

That's it. What you open and what's new for you stay on each device and are never synced.

What we don't collect

  • Reading history. Nothing about what you open or read is sent to us.
  • Click data. Stories open directly on the publisher's site. We don't proxy, log or measure the click.
  • Analytics. No Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no Mixpanel. This build includes no third-party tracking at all.
  • IP address logs. We don't keep access logs beyond what Supabase requires for security.
  • Tracking cookies. When you're signed in, your session is kept in your browser's storage. That's all.

The feed proxy

Feeds are fetched through a small helper service we run on Cloudflare, because most sites don't let a web app read their feed directly. When you add a source by its web address, the same proxy fetches that page to find its feed. The proxy:

  • Sees the address of the feed or page being fetched.
  • Does not log which person asked for which feed.
  • Does not store what it fetches.
  • Does not change what it returns.

Cloudflare sees your IP address during the request, as with any web request.

Signing in

There's no password: we email you a one-time sign-in link. Your email address is kept with Supabase, the service that handles sign-in and stores your synced sources and clippings.

You can sign out at any time from your account in the app. To delete your account entirely, get in touch and we'll remove your records from the database.

Your choices

  • Use it without signing in. Everything except clippings works without an account. Sync is optional.
  • Remove what's on this device. Remove sources in Sources, or clear your browser's data for this site.
  • Delete clippings. Use the ⋯ menu on any clipping. It's deleted from the cloud too.
  • Delete your synced data. Sign out to stop syncing, or contact us to remove your cloud records entirely.
  • Take your sources with you. Export them as an OPML file from Sources, and import them into any reader.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change what we store or how, for example by adding analytics, we'll say so in the app and in the changelog before the change takes effect.
